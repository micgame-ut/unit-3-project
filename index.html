      Weather data

      Forecasts and location search come from Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0. Place names in search come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) through Open-Meteo. When you tap "Use my location," the place name comes from BigDataCloud.

      Recommendations are playful suggestions, not safety advice.

      How recommendations work

      Only the hours you're out ("I'm out from ___ to ___") count. The outfit is picked from the warmest hourly "feels like" temperature in those hours.

      Outfit categories
      FitWarmest feels-likeBasis
      Hot90°F and upNWS heat index "extreme caution" level; loose, light layers per NWS heat safety
      Warm75–89°FOur judgment
      Mild60–74°FOur judgment
      Cool45–59°FOur judgment
      ColdBelow 45°FOur judgment for the cutoff; hat, scarf, gloves, and water-resistant coat and boots per CDC winter weather

      Reminders show when any hour you're out meets the rule:

      • Umbrella: rain chance 40% or more (our judgment).
      • Sunscreen: UV index 3 or more (EPA UV Index).
      • Water: feels like 90°F or more (NWS heat index, CDC heat health).
      • Bundle up: temperature 32°F or below (NWS wind chill, CDC winter weather).
      • Layer up: feels-like range of 20°F or more while you're out (our judgment).

      Each date gets its own randomly picked outfit and reminder wording, which stay the same when you come back to that date.

      Privacy

      • No accounts, cookies, analytics, or API keys.
      • Your location is only requested when you tap "Use my location," and it's rounded to about 1 km before it leaves your device.
      • Open-Meteo receives the city or ZIP you type, or your rounded location, to find the place and its forecast. Like most web services, it may log your IP address (Open-Meteo terms).
      • After "Use my location," BigDataCloud receives your rounded location to name your city, and may use it to improve its location data.
      • Saved only on this device: your last location, the outfit and wording picked for each date, and your usual hours out if you change them.
